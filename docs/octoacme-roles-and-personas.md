# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Personas
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on feature requirements and priorities
- **Project Managers**: Provide estimates and status updates on deliverables
- **Business Analyst**: Clarify technical feasibility and implementation details
- **QA Lead**: Work together on test automation and quality standards
- **Change Manager**: Submit change requests and provide implementation details

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Personas
- **Developers**: Define requirements and prioritize backlog items
- **Project Managers**: Align on scope, timelines, and resource needs
- **Business Analyst**: Validate business requirements and acceptance criteria
- **QA Lead**: Set quality expectations and product success metrics
- **Executive Sponsor**: Present product vision and outcomes for strategic alignment
- **Change Manager**: Evaluate change requests against product roadmap

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Personas
- **Developers**: Coordinate delivery timelines and manage dependencies
- **Product Managers**: Align on scope and priorities
- **Business Analyst**: Gather requirements and track deliverable completeness
- **QA Lead**: Monitor testing progress and quality risks
- **Change Manager**: Assess change impact on project schedule and scope
- **Executive Sponsor**: Escalate blockers and provide status updates

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions. They gather and analyze requirements, translate stakeholder expectations into actionable specifications, and ensure deliverables meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Analyze business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Translate business needs into clear, actionable technical requirements
- Create functional specifications and user stories
- Validate that deliverables align with business goals
- Facilitate requirements workshops and stakeholder meetings

### Goals
- Ensure alignment between business objectives and technical solutions
- Reduce rework by clarifying requirements upfront
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction through clear communication
- Identify opportunities for process optimization

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documents and user stories
- Stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Business process diagrams and flow charts
- Regular alignment meetings with Product Managers and developers

### Interactions with Other Personas
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate to prioritize requirements and ensure product vision alignment
- **Project Managers**: Provide detailed requirements and help estimate effort
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements and answer technical questions about business logic
- **QA Lead**: Define acceptance criteria and validation scenarios
- **Executive Sponsor**: Translate technical progress into business value metrics

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads establish and maintain quality standards across projects. They coordinate testing activities, develop testing strategies, and ensure deliverables meet quality criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and quality standards
- Coordinate test planning and execution activities
- Review and approve test cases and test data
- Verify deliverables against acceptance criteria
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigations
- Mentor team members on testing best practices
- Track and report on quality metrics

### Goals
- Maintain high product quality and reliability
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Ensure comprehensive test coverage
- Reduce time spent on bug fixes and rework

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case reviews
- Quality metrics and defect reports
- Sprint planning and retrospective participation
- Sign-off approvals before releases

### Interactions with Other Personas
- **Developers**: Review code quality, collaborate on test automation, and verify bug fixes
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria and product quality expectations
- **Project Managers**: Provide quality status updates and identify testing risks
- **Business Analyst**: Ensure test scenarios cover all business requirements
- **Change Manager**: Validate changes through comprehensive regression testing

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers oversee the change control process, ensuring that all changes to products and systems are properly evaluated, approved, documented, and communicated to affected stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Manage the change request process and change control board
- Assess impact and risk of proposed changes
- Coordinate change approval workflows
- Ensure proper documentation of all changes
- Communicate changes to affected teams and stakeholders
- Track change metrics and identify patterns

### Goals
- Minimize disruption from changes
- Ensure changes are properly vetted and approved
- Maintain clear audit trail of all changes
- Balance agility with appropriate governance

### Typical Communication
- Change request forms and impact assessments
- Change Advisory Board (CAB) meetings
- Change notifications and status updates
- Change metrics and trend reports

### Interactions with Other Personas
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate change requests and assess project impact
- **Product Managers**: Evaluate changes against product roadmap priorities
- **QA Lead**: Ensure adequate testing for changes before approval
- **Developers**: Gather technical details and implementation plans
- **Executive Sponsor**: Escalate high-risk or strategic changes for approval

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic oversight and organizational support for projects. They champion initiatives at the leadership level, remove organizational barriers, and ensure projects align with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic direction and vision alignment
- Secure funding and resources for the project
- Remove organizational blockers and escalations
- Make high-impact decisions when needed
- Champion project outcomes at executive level
- Ensure alignment with business priorities and strategy

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver strategic business value
- Enable teams to succeed by removing obstacles
- Maintain executive awareness and support
- Align projects with organizational objectives

### Typical Communication
- Strategic briefings and executive updates
- Major milestone reviews
- Escalation discussions and decision-making
- Stakeholder alignment at senior levels

### Interactions with Other Personas
- **Project Managers**: Receive status updates and escalations, provide strategic guidance
- **Product Managers**: Ensure product vision aligns with business strategy
- **Change Manager**: Review and approve major changes with strategic impact
- **Business Analyst**: Validate that business outcomes are being achieved
- **Developers**: Provide organizational context and appreciate technical achievements

# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation hub. This serves as the central entry point to all our project management processes, workflows, and best practices.

## Overview

OctoAcme's project management processes are designed to provide structure, transparency, and continuous improvement across all product initiatives. Projects begin with a clearly defined initiation phase, where teams validate ideas through business need, measurable goals, stakeholder alignment, and a lightweight Project One-pager. This upfront clarity determines whether an effort proceeds to planning, ensuring strong alignment before significant resources are committed. Once a project is approved, planning activities break the scope into actionable increments, estimate resource and timeline needs, and define acceptance criteria, risks, and a visible backlog using templates documented in the process guides.

The process defines clear roles and responsibilities for all participants. The Project Manager (PM) coordinates timelines, meetings, risks, and cross-team communication, while the Product Manager (PdM) defines outcomes and prioritizes work according to customer and business value. Developers focus on implementation, testing, and collaboration, and QA ensures delivered features meet defined quality standards. Stakeholders, both internal and external, are involved from the beginning and kept updated through structured communication cadences.

Execution is governed by iterative delivery and tracking in a shared project board with defined workflow columns (Backlog, In Progress, QA, Done, etc.). Small pull requests are linked to issues for traceability, with CI enforcement for testing and linting before code review and merging. Quality assurance is built into every stage: new logic requires unit and integration tests, automated and manual QA are performed as needed, and end-to-end smoke tests plus security scans are run prior to release. Structured checklists ensure readiness for deployment and clear rollback or incident playbooks enable fast recovery and learning from failures.

Communication and continuous improvement are cornerstones of OctoAcme's approach. Regular standups, weekly syncs, and transparent dashboards keep the entire team informed of progress, risks, and next steps. Risks are tracked in a dedicated register, reviewed weekly, with clear escalation paths. Retrospectives after every milestone or incident identify learnings and generate action items, which feed back into process improvement. This balanced process—combining structure, role clarity, communication, and relentless focus on quality—creates a resilient foundation for consistent, high-impact delivery.
